# LEXA account access rules and administration

## Existing business accounts
All workspaces present when `20261108100000_platform_admin_subscriptions` was first applied were inserted with `legacy` status. Legacy accounts remain operational without a renewal date. **Never replace the legacy backfill with a moving time-based criterion**: migrations may be deployed later on some environments, and old users must never be accidentally forced into billing. A super administrator may explicitly enrol an existing business in billing, with reason and confirmation recorded in the immutable administration audit trail.

## New business accounts
Registration and alternative business-creation flows create `pending` subscriptions. Workspace operations (including reads) are blocked by the global server-side `SubscriptionAccessGuard` until the subscription is `active` with an unexpired paid period. The billing status, payment submission and support endpoints stay reachable. A submitted payment claim **does not** activate a subscription: an authorized billing manager or super administrator must independently confirm receipt with the official payment provider. Approval creates a fixed UGX 50,000 / 30-day subscription period and audit entry. Renewal does not shorten paid time.

## Administrative controls
LEXA platform administrators sign in separately at `/platform-admin/login`. Only enabled, invited platform administrators may use the Control Centre. In **Business accounts**, permitted staff can view status, payment history, manually record verified payment, suspend/reinstate, archive/restore (data preserved), or explicitly transition a legacy business to billing. In **Users & access**, super administrators can suspend or reinstate any ACTIVE/SUSPENDED membership belonging to a specific business, without changing its role or entitlement. This includes business owners, so a confirmation and reason are required; a suspended owner's recovery requires platform administration. The operation is transactionally audited and membership changes update the platform directory via an existing database trigger. `INVITED` memberships cannot be force-activated by this route. Reinstating a user never bypasses a suspended, archived, pending, or expired business subscription.

## Release verification
Before release, run the backend account oversight and subscription policy tests, backend build and frontend typecheck/build. Confirm in a staging database that subscription rows match tenant count, all pre-existing workspaces are marked `legacy`, registration creates `pending`, payment approval and subscription rejection work, and the authenticated Control Centre displays the permitted controls. Check Render API migration deployment and Vercel `lexa_review` readiness. Do not overwrite production entitlements with a blanket `UPDATE` and do not call an unverified payment a confirmed receipt.
