Common questions

Do I need to register to try LEXA?

No. The demo creates a temporary fictional business. You can explore and make changes without entering payment details.

Will demo records become my real business records?

No. Demo records expire and are not transferred into paid accounts. Create a new business account when you are ready.

Does the demo send invoices to real customers?

No. The demo does not send external email or move real money. Financial examples are fictional.

How do I sign in to an existing business?

Open the customer sign-in page at lexaaamaal.com/sign-in. Use your existing LEXA credentials; legacy businesses do not need to register again.

When does my subscription begin?

For a new account, access begins after the payment is verified and approved. The standard period is 30 days.
