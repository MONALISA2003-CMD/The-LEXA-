LEXA MODULES

Choose a job. Find its records.

Here is what appears in the LEXA workspace, grouped the way a shop, office or operations team works.

Try these modules ↗
01 · SELLING

Checkout, sales and customers

Checkout and receipts; sales returns; quotations; sales orders; invoices; customers, contacts, leads and price lists.

Start with a customer or a product. Follow a quote through an order and receipt.
02 · STOCK

Products, stock and deliveries

Product and category records; receiving; stock counts; batches; transfers; warehouses; picklists; bundles; packages and deliveries.

See quantities against the business branch and trace stock movements.
03 · PURCHASING

Suppliers and purchase work

Purchase requests; requests for quotation; supplier quotes; purchase orders; receiving; supplier invoices and returns.

Follow a supplier order from request through receipt and payable.
04 · FINANCE

Money, accounting and tax

Expenses; cash sessions; money accounts; receipts and payments; journals; chart of accounts; reconciliation; budgets; fixed assets and tax documents.

Check source transactions before reviewing account balances and reports.
05 · PEOPLE

Staff and payroll

Employee records; attendance; shifts; leave; timesheets; payroll and employee permissions.

Assign access according to each person’s role.
06 · OPERATIONS

Production, service and projects

Bills of materials; work orders; job cards; quality checks; projects; tasks and customer service cases.

Keep the job, materials and responsible people on the same record.
07 · OVERSIGHT

Business dashboard and reports

Business overview; reporting; LEXA Assistant; account settings; business profile; access history and approved integrations.

Review figures supported by actual transactions and permissions.

Available actions depend on the business setup, user permissions and the current product release. The demo lets you check supported screens and transactions without using a real account.
