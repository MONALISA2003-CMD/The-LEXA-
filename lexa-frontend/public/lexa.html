BEFORE YOU START

Good questions. Straight answers.

What happens in the sample, what stays private and how real business access works.

More help and contact details ↗
Can I try LEXA without registering?+

Yes. Open the browser-based sample business. It contains fictional data, uses the same LEXA workspace screens, and never sends your changes to a customer account.

Is the demo the same application used by customers?+

The browser demo runs LEXA’s business pages and local business record adapters. The real service adds server-backed accounts, staff permissions, synchronization and integrations. Actions requiring the live service are unavailable in the sample.

Can I explore finance, payroll and purchasing?+

Yes. The sample business includes named fictional records across business areas, plus linked checkout receipts, stock movements and payment entries. Some connected services and administrator-only functions are intentionally disabled.

Will I need to pay to try the sample?+

No. The sample is free and needs no card. A live LEXA business account follows the subscription and activation steps described on the Pricing page.

Can I use LEXA on a phone?+

Yes. The interface is responsive for phones, tablets and desktop browsers. The sample records you create in your browser do not automatically synchronize to other devices.

What happens to changes I make in the demo?+

The sample is kept only in this browser, for a maximum of two hours per visit. You can restart it whenever you like. Closing the sample does not affect customer or production data.
